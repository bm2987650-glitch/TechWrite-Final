Contributor Guides

Learn how to publish articles and join our platform content team.

How to Become Part of Our Team

We are always looking for passionate technology writers, developers, and AI enthusiasts to contribute to TechWrite. Here is how you can apply:

  • Submit an Application: Go to our Contact page and select "Join Team" as your message topic.
  • Share Your Portfolio: Provide links to your previous blog posts, GitHub profile, or writing samples.
  • Review Process: Our editorial team reviews applications within 3 business days. If approved, you will receive login credentials for the publishing dashboard.

How to Publish an Article

Follow these steps to submit and publish an article on TechWrite:

  1. Sign In: Log in to your creator account via the Sign In page.
  2. Draft Your Content: Use our dashboard editor to write your title, paste Markdown text, and add appropriate category tags (e.g., AI Tools, Tutorials).
  3. Add Visuals: Ensure images are high quality, properly credited, and responsive.
  4. Submit for Review: Click "Submit Draft". An editor will review the content for accuracy and formatting before going live.
